# Week 08 — Diagrams and lists

**Module:** Algorithmic Problem Solving (`COMP H1034`)  
**Semester:** 1 (Year 1), folder `sem01`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lab on diagrams and lists as problem-solving tools. The sheet has five diagram problems (a bouncing ball, a worm on a wall, a race, handshakes, a rock climb), five systematic-list problems (cards and comics, a tennis round robin, the orders of concert calls, four even numbers that add up to 24, two numbers with a product of 360) and three classic puzzles, of which the sheet asks for any two. The solution has a diagram for each of the five diagram problems and gives the lists and the answers to all three classic puzzles.
