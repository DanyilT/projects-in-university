## What this module is for

Personal and Professional Development covers presentations, communication skills and teamwork. It starts with an extended induction in week 1 (online preparation tasks about the student union, class representatives and equality, and a quiz). The main assessment is a group presentation (free subject): report and a presentation on blockchain technology, its applications and how to hack a blockchain. Lab 5 (week 8) is a climate change report of seven pages. Week 11 is an MCQ test, which is not in the repository. This folder has the climate change report, the links to the blockchain report and presentation on their branch, and screenshots of those files. The blockchain files themselves are on the branch.
