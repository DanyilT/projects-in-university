# Week 03 — Lab 3: planning and scoping a penetration test

**Module:** Professional Penetration Testing (`DFCS H3014`)  
**Semester:** 6 (Year 3), folder `sem06`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lab 3 (2 hours, 16 February 2026, submitted on 23 February) is a planning and scoping exercise on a scenario: ACME Health Systems, a mid-sized medical services provider with several clinics that processes personal and sensitive data (patient demographics, appointment details and basic clinical notes), asks for an external and a limited internal penetration test to prepare for an audit; GDPR applies and no service disruption is permitted Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. The lab pack (Student Pack & Worksheet) has four worksheets: A, scope definition, B, rules of engagement (RoE), C, methodology selection and justification, and D, a mini scoping report. The completed sections are submitted as a single Word document in the next week's lab.
