# Week 10 — Lab 10: privilege escalation

**Module:** Professional Penetration Testing (`DFCS H3014`)  
**Semester:** 6 (Year 3), folder `sem06`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lab 10 (3 hours, 20 April 2026, submitted on 8 May) is two TryHackMe rooms on privilege escalation, which depends on the configuration of the target (the kernel version, the installed applications, the supported languages, other users' passwords): Linux PrivEsc Arena, a very vulnerable Linux VM with SSH open, and Linux Privilege Escalation, the fundamentals from enumeration to exploitation with over eight techniques. The drop-in tutorial of the week (23 April 2026) was a chance to practise pentesting on target machines to prepare for the exam; its screen recording (41 minutes, black frames removed) is on [MEGA](https://mega.nz/file/Twh1DIgQ#LAlxsrFbH1D_bXpY3p7GmijtLVeYrW6x__Wkf1LdZ88) and listed in [Screen recordings](../README.md#screen-recordings). The lab page asks to understand each attack, because the steps are needed for the practical exam.
