# Week 09 — Lab 9: cracking wireless passwords

**Module:** Professional Penetration Testing (`DFCS H3014`)  
**Semester:** 6 (Year 3), folder `sem06`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lab 9 (3 hours, 13 April 2026, submitted on 8 May) shows how simple it is to break WEP and join a WLAN or sniff its traffic with the aircrack suite on Kali Linux: connecting a wireless adapter to the Kali VM, putting the card in monitor mode, scanning and capturing local wireless traffic (airodump-ng), cracking WEP networks and cracking WPA/WPA2 networks, with a summary of the commands and a flowchart of the steps. The lab/resources folder has the capture made in the lab, and the resources folder has the guide to installing Microsoft Network Monitor to test a WiFi adapter.
