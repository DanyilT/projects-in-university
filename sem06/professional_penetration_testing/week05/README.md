## What this week is for

Lab 6 (360 minutes, 2 March 2026, submitted on 16 March) is a first look at vulnerability scanners, with the two most popular free options, Nessus and OpenVAS, to try both and choose one: Vulnerabilities 101 (the flaws of an application and research in vulnerability databases), setting up and using Nessus, the basics of threat and vulnerability management with OpenVAS (Greenbone), and scanning Metasploitable 1. The installation guides of Nessus and OpenVAS are in the module resources.
