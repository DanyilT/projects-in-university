# Week 04 — Lab 4: meeting management

**Module:** IT Business Management (`COMP H2034`)  
**Semester:** 4 (Year 2), folder `sem04`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Week 4 was meeting management (MCQ 3). The lab asks the team to hold a meeting about the web server and software testing projects, to record the minutes in the template (an Excel sheet), to upload the minutes to Moodle (every member), and to keep a space for the minutes of the meetings on the Trello board. The resources are a competency framework, a Big Five personality test result and the Belbin team role descriptions. The solution is the minutes of the team's first meeting (Monday 3 March 2025, 9am), which prepared for it with an updated lab 4 document with annotated use cases and a working demo of the database connection, and discussed the state of the project and the software engineering and web server CAs.
