## What this week is for

The review lecture of 24 November 2025 (13:00 to 15:00, Michael Hegarty) recapped the module. The notes cover the forensics process (identify, preserve, acquire, analyse and discover, document and present), chain of custody, the role of the investigator, bit-stream copies and hashing, the secure lab and write blockers, validated tools (ISO 27037), storage fundamentals (slack space and unallocated space), scope creep and documenting evidence.
