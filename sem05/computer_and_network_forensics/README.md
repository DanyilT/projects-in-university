## What this module is for

Computer and Network Forensics is the third-year digital forensics module. It goes through the forensic process (identify, preserve, acquire, analyse and discover, document and present), write blockers, volatile data and live forensics, forensic duplicates with FTK Imager, analysis with FTK and Autopsy, steganography, file carving with a hex editor, file signatures (magic numbers), the legal side (the ISO standards for digital evidence and the Irish cybercrime legislation), and forensic reports with the chain of custody. Each week has a lecture and a lab, and the labs are answered in Word or PDF documents that are uploaded to Brightspace. Week 4 had no lecture or labs and week 7 was a reading week. This folder holds the lab sheets and the evidence images of the labs, the answers and forensic analysis reports (solution), the readings, the learning plan, the notes of the review lecture, and the two assessments: the Lab 7 technical challenge (a forensic investigation report on a disk image) and the CA 3 research paper with its presentation. Labs 8 and 9 (memory forensics and network forensics) are on Brightspace but are not in the weekly plan, so their sheets are in [`resources/`](resources/). The module is 100% continuous assessment: ongoing lab work (CA 1, 20%), a multiple choice quiz (CA 2, 40%, 1 December 2025, 40 questions in 40 minutes) and a research paper (CA 3, 40%, due at 18:00 on 7 December 2025) with presentations on 8 December 2025.
