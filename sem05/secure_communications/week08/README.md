## What this week is for

The lecture of week 8 was cryptanalysis and attacks. Lab 3 (Hashing) compares the hashing methods (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-3, bcrypt and PBKDF2), their strength and the use of salting. Section A generates, compares and verifies hash values with OpenSSL and shows the role of salts and of hash comparisons for file integrity; section B uses Hashcat for dictionary and brute-force attacks, benchmarks the speed of the algorithms and shows how the complexity of a password and the type of hash affect cracking. Each section is worth 1 mark (2 in total); the PDF is uploaded to Brightspace by Sunday 9 November 2025, 23:59.
