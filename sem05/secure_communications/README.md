## What this module is for

Secure Communications is the third-year cryptography module. It goes through classic ciphers, encoding and hashing, keys and symmetric and asymmetric encryption, key exchange (Diffie-Hellman), RSA, elliptic curves and digital signatures, PKI, certificates and SSL/TLS, cryptanalysis, wireless security and PGP. The weekly labs follow the eSecurity labs of Bill Buchanan (asecuritysite.com): ciphers and fundamentals, symmetric key (OpenSSL, AES and DES padding, Python), hashing and Hashcat, trust and digital certificates, and tunnelling and web security (SSL/TLS, VPNs and Wireshark traces). Each lab is marked out of 2 marks (3 in week 5): the first two labs are submitted to GitHub Classroom and, from week 4, the lab report is one PDF (a title page, the completed tables and screenshots of the commands and output) uploaded to Brightspace by the Sunday of the same week. Week 7 had no lab. This folder holds the lab sheets, the filled sheets, reports and submitted images (solution), the README files of the GitHub Classroom repositories of the first two labs, the course summary, and Assessment 1 (the CryptoHack project) with its brief, the submitted PDF and the CryptoHack screenshots. The module is 20% labs, 40% project and 40% in-class test; the project is Assessment 1, the CryptoHack project (weeks 4 to 6, due on 2 November 2025), and Assessment 2, the Challenge Labs (25%, weeks 9 to 12, due on 7 December 2025, any four of six challenge labs), which is on the matching project branch.

## Assessments and other files

Assessment 1 is the CryptoHack project (weeks 4 to 6, deadline 2 November 2025, 23:59, a target of about 500 points): CryptoHack challenges are solved and two parts are submitted to GitHub Classroom, one PDF with a screenshot of the final CryptoHack score (username and total score) and a screenshot of the solved challenges page, and a repository with one folder per challenge, named like the challenge, with the source code or a short explanation. The rubric is the CryptoHack score (60%), code quality and organisation (30%) and the report (10%). Assessment 2, the Challenge Labs (25%, weeks 9 to 12, deadline 7 December 2025, 23:59), is to complete any four of six challenge labs (Many Time Pad, Encrypted Image, Breaking Salted Hashes, Encrypted Evidence, Encrypted Wireless Capture and Simple Blockchain); its files and solutions are on the matching project branch. The submission has solutions for 22 challenges (one folder per challenge with the solution code, and the full report with the solutions); they are kept in the private GitHub Classroom repository and are not shown publicly, so they are not in this repository. At the time of the screenshots the solved challenges were Introduction 3 of 3, General 19 of 19 and Mathematics 8 of 15. The in-class test is not in the repository.
