## What this week is for

The lecture of week 5 was RSA, elliptic curve cryptography and digital signatures. Lab 2 (Symmetric Key) introduces block ciphers, stream ciphers and padding with OpenSSL, Python and JavaScript. Week 5 covers three sections: A, OpenSSL (the supported encryption methods and the OpenSSL version, prime tests, encrypting and decrypting a text file, including Blowfish in CBC mode), B, AES padding, and C, DES padding (how the block size affects the length of the ciphertext). The lab is marked out of 3, one mark per section; the PDF has a title page, the completed tables and answers and screenshots of the commands and output, and is uploaded to Brightspace by Sunday 19 October 2025, 23:59.
