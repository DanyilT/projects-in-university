# Week 09 — Lab 4: trust and digital certificates (sections A and B)

**Module:** Secure Communications (`DFCS H3013`)  
**Semester:** 5 (Year 3), folder `sem05`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

The lecture of week 9 was wireless security. Lab 4 (Trust and Digital Certificates) is about the trust infrastructure of PKI: section A reads real digital certificates (serial number, effective date, name, issuer and so on) and evaluates the trust issues, and section B creates and manages certificates with OpenSSL (setting up a PKI and generating certificates). Each section is worth 1 mark (2 in total); the PDF is uploaded to Brightspace by Sunday 16 November 2025, 23:59.
