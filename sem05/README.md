# Year 3 | Semester 1

| Module | Short description | Assessment / project | Tests | CAs | Labs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Computer and Network Forensics](computer_and_network_forensics) | Digital forensics: the forensic process, write blockers, volatile data, imaging with FTK Imager, analysis with FTK and Autopsy, steganography, file carving with a hex editor, the legal side and forensic reports. | [CA 3: research paper on steganalysis and presentation (team of three, 40%)](computer_and_network_forensics/README.md#assessments-and-other-files), due 7 December 2025; the Lab 7 technical challenge is a forensic investigation report | MCQ (CA 2, 1 December 2025, 40%) | — | Labs 1 to 7 (CA 1, 20%) |
| [Secure Communications](secure_communications) | Cryptography: classic ciphers, encoding and hashing, symmetric key, key exchange, RSA and digital signatures, certificates and PKI, SSL/TLS and tunnelling, with the eSecurity labs. | [Project (40%)](secure_communications/README.md#assessments-and-other-files): Assessment 1, the CryptoHack project (weeks 4 to 6, 2 November 2025), and Assessment 2, the Challenge Labs (25%, weeks 9 to 12, 7 December 2025) | In-class test (40%) | — | Weekly labs (20%) |
| [Secure Programming](secure_programming) | Secure coding and web application security: threat modelling, injection, cross-site scripting, access control, CSRF, command injection and static code analysis, on a vulnerable Django application. | [Assignment 1: find, exploit and fix the vulnerabilities of a Flask application (group of 2 to 3, 40%)](secure_programming/README.md#assessments-and-other-files), due 16 November 2025 | MCQ test (12 December 2025) | — | Weekly labs, including the bug bounty session |

## Semester calendar

Semester 1 of 2025-26 started on 15 September 2025 (week 1). Week 7 (27 October) was a review week, week 13 started on 8 December, the winter break was from 15 December and the assessments and exams began on 5 January 2026 (the TU Dublin academic calendar 2025-2026).
