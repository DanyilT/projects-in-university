# Week 05 — Lab 5: normalisation to third normal form (continued)

**Module:** Database Fundamentals (`COMP H1029`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lab 5 repeats the exercises of lab 4 and adds two more: Exercise 3 normalises a transaction table and Exercise 4 normalises a table of students and their advisors. The sheet makes lab 5 mandatory, and labs 4 and 5 were submitted in one document. The lecture was SQL functions. The diagrams of Exercises 1 to 3 are the same as in week 4.
