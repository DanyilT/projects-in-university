# Week 06 — Lab 5: configure a wireless network for SOHO (Packet Tracer)

**Module:** LAN Switching and Wireless (`COMP H2015`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 6 was WLAN concepts. Lab 5 is a Packet Tracer activity on a small office or home office network: connect to a wireless router, update the default router settings, configure the wireless settings, and connect wireless clients to an access point.
