# Week 09 — Tutorial 8: matrices, part 2

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 8 was matrices, part 2. Tutorial 8 covers transposes of matrices, identity matrices, and the determinants and inverses of 2 by 2 and 3 by 3 matrices. The resources are worked notes from the lecturer: a recap of part 1, matrix multiplication, the 2 by 2 determinant and inverse, the matrix of minors and cofactors, the 3 by 3 determinant (also a short-cut way, with two examples) and the 3 by 3 inverse.
