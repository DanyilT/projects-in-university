# Week 11 — Tutorials 10 and 11: integration and recursion

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 10 was integration and lecture 11 was recursion. The integration sheet asks for integrals of powers and polynomials and for the area bounded by a curve. The recursion sheet asks what recursion is and why it is useful, evaluating recursively defined sequences, recursive and non-recursive definitions of sequences, and an iterative algorithm for the first terms of a sequence. The folder has both sheets, the lecturer's sample solutions and the integration lecture exercises.
