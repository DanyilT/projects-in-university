# Week 04 — Tutorial 4: statistics, part 2

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 4 continued statistics. Tutorial 4 has two sections. Section A is about graphs: a histogram of a frequency table with a comment on its skewness, a time series plot of the number of computing students from 1970 to 2000, and scatter plots with the type and strength of the correlation. Section B is numerical calculations on grouped data: the mean, standard deviation, median and quartiles, with question 6 shown as an example. The folder has the sheet and the lecturer's sample solutions for both sections.
