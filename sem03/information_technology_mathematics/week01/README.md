# Week 01 — Tutorial 1: graphs

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 1 was graph theory and tutorial 1 practises it: drawing undirected and directed graphs from their edges and from adjacency matrices, checking that the degrees add up to twice the number of edges, writing adjacency matrices, finding Hamiltonian circuits and paths, and the order of visiting vertices in a breadth-first and a depth-first search. The outline schedules tutorial 1 for week 2. The folder has the sheet and the lecturer's sample solutions.
