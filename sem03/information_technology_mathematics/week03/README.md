# Week 03 — Tutorial 3: statistics, part 1

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 3 was statistics: sources and collection of data, sampling and describing data. The tutorial asks for the differences between categorical and continuous data, primary and secondary data, random and stratified random sampling, and observational and experimental data; why a sample is used; a frequency table with a bar chart; and a histogram of the time that students spend logged in. The sheet is headed Tutorial 4 - Statistics Part 1. The folder has the sheet, an overview of it and the lecturer's sample solutions.
