# Week 05 — Tutorial 5: probability

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 5 was probability, in two parts over weeks 5 and 6 (probability, then probability distributions). Tutorial 5 starts with dice (single dice, the sum and the product of two dice with an event space, the complement rule), then the probability that two of 15 people pick the same number, independent events, permutations and combinations, and counting codes (section A), and probability distributions (section B). The folder has the sheet and the lecturer's sample solutions for part 1 and part 2.
