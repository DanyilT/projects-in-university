# Week 10 — Tutorial 9: differentiation

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)

[Module README](../README.md)

## What this week is for

Lecture 9 was differential calculus. Tutorial 9 has 14 questions, from the average rate of change to derivatives (including the product and quotient rules) and the tests for maximum and minimum points. The folder has the sheet, an overview of part 2 (the first and second derivative tests), the lecturer's sample solutions for questions 1 to 9 and 10 to 14, and a sheet of steps for finding maxima, minima and points of inflection.
