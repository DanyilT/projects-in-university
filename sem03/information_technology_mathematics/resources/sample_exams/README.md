# Sample exams

**Module:** Information Technology Mathematics (`COMP H2026`)  
**Semester:** 3 (Year 2), folder `sem03`

[Module README](../../README.md)

## What this folder is for

The January exam papers of the module for 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023 and 2024, the lecturer's sample solutions, and the revision list. Every paper is worth 100 marks: question 1 is compulsory and has eight parts of 5 marks (40 marks), and two of the other three questions are answered (30 marks each). The other questions are differentiation and integration, statistics and probability, and matrices, graphs and trees; the 2020 paper has statistics and probability, matrices and recursion, and graphs and trees. Each paper ends with the formula sheets and the table of normal probabilities. The solutions are scanned handwritten pages: one file for the whole 2018 paper, one file for each question of the 2019, 2020, 2022 and 2024 papers, and one file for each group of topics of the 2023 paper.
